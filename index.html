Akshay Singhal

Postdoctoral Fellow

Department of Civil, Construction, and Environmental Engineering

The University of Alabama

iD CV

My research integrates physical hydrology and data-driven methods to understand and assess the impacts of hydrological extremes, with a primary focus on floods. I develop impact-based forecasting frameworks that combine machine learning, physical models, and large-scale social data to move beyond hazard prediction toward actionable estimates of real-world consequences. My long-term vision is to build human-centered forecasting systems that support earlier, more informed, and equitable decision-making.
